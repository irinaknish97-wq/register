i. What is register?

Register is how formal or casual your language is, chosen to fit who you’re talking to, where and why. None is “better”; each fits a different moment. A student who tells a client “Hey mate, gimme the report” isn’t making a grammar mistake. It’s a register one. Pick a situation and hear how real people would say it:

What changes between registers?

Vocabulary (purchase → buy → grab) · grammar and structure (Might I suggest… → Why don’t we…) · contractions and ellipsis (I am → I’m → Running late) · phrasal vs. single verbs (postpone → put off) · pronunciation and connected speech (going to → gonna) · tone and punctuation in writing.

Neutral and informal / casual overlap, so think of register as a scale rather than four boxes: neutral is safe with almost anyone, informal / casual is for people you know and relaxed settings.
